\begin{abstract}
We propose a localization-sensitive universal property for ends and coends of
category-valued data indexed over a Grothendieck site.  The definition is made
for a pseudofunctor of coefficients and specializes to the canonical stack of
a sheaf topos.  Its cowedges range over every generalized object above a slice
site, rather than over a single fibre.  We prove the representing property,
uniqueness, functorial restriction to slices, invariance under indexed
equivalence, and reduction to ordinary coends over the terminal site.  We then
give an explicit generators-and-relations coequalizer for every fixed base
object.  If the site category has chosen pullbacks, the coefficients have
indexed coproducts, and reindexing preserves the displayed colimits, this
formula is stable under base change.  A pullback-deficient site shows why the
extra base hypothesis cannot simply be omitted.  Finally, under the precise
hypothesis that the inner relative coends exist stably, we prove a Fubini
theorem by identifying iterated and simultaneous relative cowedges.  Relative
co-Yoneda, profunctor composition, and site-presentation invariance remain
open stages of the programme.
\end{abstract}
